Industry, energy & logistics

Factories, plants, ports and fleets already generate the signals that matter. The work is turning them into something a supervisor can act on before an incident, not after it.

  • Factories
  • Oil & gas
  • Power plants
  • Ports
  • Airports
  • Transport & fleets

What usually goes wrong

Safety, quality and maintenance data arrives late, in three different formats, and usually after the event it was supposed to prevent. The people closest to the risk are the ones with the least time to write it down.

Occupational safety HSE

  • Live video analysis for missing protective equipment, falls and restricted-zone entry, with alerts tied to a specific area.
  • A safety officer dictates an incident report by voice, in their own dialect, and receives a formatted report ready for management.
  • Hands-free operation through connected smart helmets, for environments where stopping to type is not an option.

Production & quality

  • Defect detection on the line, so waste is caught in the batch rather than at the customer.
  • Automatic reading of analogue and digital gauges, with operators alerted to abnormal values.
  • Predictive maintenance that reads early signals and schedules the stop instead of suffering it.

Logistics & sites

  • Shipment tracking and perimeter monitoring for sensitive facilities.
  • Traffic and lane-violation analysis across yards and access roads.
  • Thermal imaging for fire, overheating and leak detection before the event becomes an emergency.

Where the boundary sits

Vision systems support the safety process; they do not replace it. Detection thresholds, camera placement and escalation rules are agreed with your HSE team, and deployment needs on-site testing.

Real estate & managed communities

Developers and community operators lose more to slow replies and unrecorded maintenance than to the market. Both are workflow problems.

  • Development
  • Property marketing
  • Community management
  • Residential & commercial

What usually goes wrong

Enquiries arrive across several listing platforms at once, sales teams answer the loudest rather than the closest to buying, and the reason a client walked away is never written down anywhere useful.

Marketing & sales

  • Connected listing platforms with instant answers on price, area and payment plans.
  • Lead scoring that tells the sales team which conversations are worth a call today.
  • Rejection reasons captured and analysed, so the pattern behind lost deals becomes visible.

Community operations

  • Camera monitoring that flags unfamiliar vehicles and access to restricted areas outside working hours.
  • A maintenance supervisor records notes by voice while walking lighting, lifts and landscaping; the report reaches the operational system before the walk ends.

Where the boundary sits

Scoring guides attention, it does not decide. Pricing, availability and any commitment to a client stay with a person.

Hospitality, tourism & public spaces

Hotels, venues, retail and healthcare share one problem: a request in the wrong language, at the wrong desk, at the busiest hour.

  • Hotels
  • Travel
  • Malls & retail
  • Hospitals
  • Museums

What usually goes wrong

Reception becomes a bottleneck for requests it was never meant to process, and language turns a two-minute task into a twenty-minute one.

Hotel operations

  • An in-room assistant that takes housekeeping, food and maintenance requests and routes them straight to the responsible team.
  • Instant translation of a guest request into the language of the staff member who will act on it.
  • Room preparation ordered by arrival time and guest status rather than by room number.

Travel & tourism

  • Draft itineraries and budgets built from a traveller’s stated interests, before a consultant spends an hour on them.
  • A profile and readiness summary prepared before the sales call, not during it.
  • A companion experience for visitors: local prices, transport, regulations, currency and live translation in one place.

Retail & healthcare

  • Loss prevention, footfall analysis and crowd-density alerts for public areas.
  • Fall detection for elderly and at-risk patients under camera supervision.
  • Protocol compliance checks for sterilisation and protective equipment in clinical areas.

Where the boundary sits

Anything involving patients, minors or biometric identification needs a legal and clinical review in the deployment country before it is built, not after.

Professional & legal services

Formation offices, law firms and consultancies run on documents, dates and follow-up—three things that degrade quietly when they live in a spreadsheet.

  • Law firms
  • Company formation
  • Consulting
  • Corporate teams

What usually goes wrong

Client documents arrive incomplete, renewal dates are tracked by whoever remembers them, and the same qualifying questions get asked in every first meeting.

Formation & compliance

  • An assistant that reads the client’s intended activity and outlines the entity options, with the trade-offs stated plainly.
  • Document collection and verification tracked digitally, so a missing signature is caught at intake rather than at submission.
  • A dashboard of every case stage, with automatic notice before licences, residencies and banking obligations expire.

Running the practice

  • The same workflow layer covers intake, finance and recruitment instead of three disconnected tools.
  • Renewal and follow-up become a scheduled conversation rather than an emergency.

Where the boundary sits

The system prepares and tracks. It does not give legal advice, and no filing leaves the office without a qualified person approving it.

Workforce training & knowledge transfer

This layer sits inside every sector above. New staff need answers without interrupting a senior colleague; technicians need guidance in the moment, on the equipment.

  • Internal capability for every sector above

What usually goes wrong

Operating manuals are complete and unread. The knowledge people actually use lives with a handful of senior staff, and it walks out with them.

Ask the manual Q&A

  • Operating procedures, curricula and policies become answerable by voice or text, with the source passage attached to every answer.
  • New staff get unblocked without pulling an experienced colleague off the line.

Guided inspection Field

  • Step-by-step voice guidance through complex maintenance, so the procedure is followed in the order it was written.

Simulation AR / VR

  • Vision tracking inside the simulation confirms the trainee followed the correct sequence, and corrects an unsafe action while it happens.

Where the boundary sits

This supports training. It is not a certification programme, and it does not replace the assessment your regulator recognises.
