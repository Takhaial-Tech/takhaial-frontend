Automate & integrate

AI & workflow automation

A request should not need five people to copy it between five tools. We design workflows that move information, trigger the right action and surface the decisions that need a person.

Start with the workflow, not the model.

We map what happens today: inputs, owners, systems, exceptions and the cost of waiting. Then we define a focused first release. Deterministic rules handle predictable steps; AI helps interpret language and unstructured content. Human approval remains where the consequences matter.

What it looks like

  • Route incoming inquiries to the right team with their context attached.
  • Extract document fields and send uncertain results for review.
  • Connect requests, approvals, task creation and status updates.

What you get

  • Workflow map and prioritised opportunity brief
  • Connected pilot with review and exception paths
  • Integration, permissions and evaluation plan
  • Handover, operating guide and improvement backlog
  1. 01Discover the bottleneck
  2. 02Build one useful flow
  3. 03Test the exceptions
  4. 04Launch, observe, improve

Software & systems integration

Build the interfaces and connections a workflow actually needs: internal tools, portals, APIs and shared operational views.

A useful system is more than a dashboard.

We start with the actions people need to take. Data models, permissions, integration boundaries and error handling are designed with the interface. The result can sit alongside your current tools instead of asking the whole company to start again.

What it looks like

  • Connect forms, CRM records, approvals and notifications.
  • Build a focused internal tool around a repeated operational task.
  • Give field and office teams a shared workflow with clear ownership.

What you get

  • Product and data-flow specification
  • Web interface and API integrations
  • Role and permission design
  • Testing, release and handover documentation
  1. 01Understand the systems
  2. 02Define the data contract
  3. 03Build the usable core
  4. 04Test end to end

Understand & assist

AI assistants & agents

Help customers and teams find answers, qualify a request or complete a task. Connect the conversation to approved knowledge and the tools that can act on it.

Helpful answers. Explicit boundaries.

We define what the assistant can know, what it may do and when it must hand over. Tool access is scoped to the job. Actions that change business records or contact customers can require approval. The experience includes uncertainty and escalation, not just the happy path.

What it looks like

  • A website assistant that gathers requirements before handing over to sales.
  • A service assistant that answers from approved material and routes exceptions.
  • A guided assistant inside a product or an immersive experience.

What you get

  • Conversation and escalation design
  • Knowledge and tool integrations
  • Evaluation scenarios and review controls
  • Web or application interface and handover
  1. 01Define the job
  2. 02Connect trusted context
  3. 03Set action boundaries
  4. 04Evaluate real conversations

Knowledge & document intelligence

Company knowledge lives in documents, inboxes and people’s heads. Build a way to find the right context, see its source and turn it into the next step.

An answer should lead back to evidence.

We organise source material, design access boundaries and connect retrieval to an actual task. That might be answering an internal question, checking a document or preparing a draft. We plan for outdated files, incomplete evidence and questions the system should not answer.

What it looks like

  • Search policies and working documents with source references.
  • Prepare a structured brief from a set of approved documents.
  • Extract, classify and review incoming forms or reports.

What you get

  • Source inventory and access map
  • Document preparation and retrieval workflow
  • Answer interface with references and review
  • Quality checks and source-update process
  1. 01Inventory sources
  2. 02Structure and connect
  3. 03Ask with context
  4. 04Review and maintain

Build & experience

AI-assisted creative pipelines

Build a repeatable creative workflow around references, 3D guidance, generation and human review. Use AI where it supports a usable production result.

The workflow matters as much as the output.

We combine real-time and technical-art experience with AI-assisted production techniques. Camera planning, source assets, consistent references and quality checks help connect an experiment to a practical creative process. The right workflow depends on the content and the tools you already use.

What it looks like

  • Guide generative shots with a planned3D camera and scene.
  • Develop visual concepts with consistent references and review.
  • Bring generated assets into a Unity production workflow.

What you get

  • Workflow and toolchain design
  • A focused visual or pipeline prototype
  • Reference, asset and review guidance
  • A handover with clear quality boundaries
  1. 01Set the visual brief
  2. 02Build the guidance
  3. 03Generate and review
  4. 04Prepare for production

XR & spatial experiences

Virtual showrooms, spatial product demonstrations and interactive training make complex ideas tangible. This is part of our engineering foundation—and a platform for future AI-enabled experiences.

Use immersion where it earns its place.

We choose between VR, AR and mixed reality around the audience, device and environment. Interaction, 3D content, performance and deployment are considered together. AI can be added where a guide, conversational interface or connected workflow makes the experience more useful.

What it looks like

  • A virtual product environment people can explore.
  • An augmented-reality product demonstration in a physical space.
  • A training simulation with guided actions and feedback.

What you get

  • Experience concept and interaction design
  • Real-time 3D content and application
  • Device-ready build and performance checks
  • Content update and deployment guidance
  1. 01Define the experience
  2. 02Prototype the interaction
  3. 03Build the world
  4. 04Test on the target device
